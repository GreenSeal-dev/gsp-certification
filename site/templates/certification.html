Certification

The practices are open to everyone. Certification against them is operated by GreenSeal.dev, the only body that can certify organisations or teams against GSP.

GSP certifies organisational practices, not individual products. It applies to software-intensive systems, cloud-native applications and AI/ML workloads, and is meant for technology companies, SaaS providers, organisations running AI/ML workloads, and engineering teams that want to reduce energy use, cost and carbon impact.

Which version of GSP

Each certification is assessed against a specific published version of GSP, such as v{{ latest.version }}. A later version doesn't change the version an existing certification was granted against. Renewals are assessed against the version current at the time of renewal. Every version stays online on the versions page.

Badges and verification

Certified organisations display their badge with a link to their own verification page on this site. The badge may only be used as provided and while the certification is valid. Misuse or misleading claims can lead to revocation.

Certification is subject to the Terms of Certification. Questions about certification: info@greenseal.dev.

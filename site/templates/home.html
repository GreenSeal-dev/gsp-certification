How to read a practice

ID
Category code and number. It stays the same when a practice's levels change.
Requirement
The statement that is assessed, written with shall.
Rationale
Why the practice matters.
Evidence examples
What can show the practice is in place. Other evidence may also be accepted.
Assessment scale
The options an assessor chooses from, such as Implemented or Partially Implemented.

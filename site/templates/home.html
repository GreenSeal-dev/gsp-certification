How to read a practice

GSP-MEA-04
A permanent ID: category code and number. IDs don't change when a practice's levels do.
Requirement
The normative statement, written with shall. This is what gets assessed.
Rationale and evidence
Informative. Why the practice matters, and examples of evidence that can show it is in place.
Assessment scale
The options an assessor chooses from, each with a score from 1 to 0.
Priority and weight
Core or Supporting, and the weight that multiplies the score. How scoring works.

Use and cite

You may use, share and adapt the practices, including commercially, under CC BY 4.0. Credit "GreenSeal.dev (https://greenseal.dev)" and say if you made changes.

Cite the version you used. Each practice page has a citation for that practice, and the frozen URL {{ base_url }}/v{{ v.version }}/ always shows this exact text.

Certification

The practices are open, but only GreenSeal.dev can certify organisations or teams against GSP. Using or adapting the practices does not make an organisation GSP certified.

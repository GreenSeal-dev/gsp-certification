How to read a practice

ID
Each practice has a permanent ID made of its category code and a number, such as GSP-MEA-04. The ID stays the same when a practice's levels change.
Requirement
The statement that is assessed, written with shall.
Rationale
Why the practice matters.
Evidence examples
Examples of evidence that can show the practice is in place. Other evidence may also be accepted.
Assessment scale
The options an assessor chooses from, such as Implemented, Partially Implemented or Not Implemented.
Priority
Core practices are central to their category. Supporting practices strengthen it.
